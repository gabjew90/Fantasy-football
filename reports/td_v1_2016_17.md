# anytime_td_v1: layer 2 and the end-to-end test

Tune [2022, 2023], test [2016, 2017]. Population: QB/RB/WR/TE on the game-day active list, 16747 player-games in test, 12.1% of whom scored. Active list covers 95.7% of snaps; 43.0% of actives hold a depth-chart slot. Five channels on raw counts; reallocation 'all'; kappa 5; moved-role weight 0.25.
