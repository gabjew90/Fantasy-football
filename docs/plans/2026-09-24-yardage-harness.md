# Yardage props to the touchdown model's standard

The anytime-TD model was tuned on 2022-23 and tested on 2024-25, walk-forward,
against the version before it. The yardage markets were not:

| Market | Evidence today |
|---|---|
| receptions, receiving yards (`receiving_hier_v2`) | one season (2025 weeks 9-18); a known 4.5% volume bias on 2024 |
| rushing yards (`rush_yds_v0`) | none |
| QB passing yards | not modelled |

This plan replaces step 7 of the consolidation plan (the QB-out rule), which
becomes one question inside it.

## Step 1 -- one harness for every yardage market (this PR)

**Question:** how good are the yardage distributions the live scorer prices,
season by season, and where are they biased?

- **Grade the sampler that ships.** Rushing yards are drawn in `score_game.py`
  inline; the draw moves into `model.simulate_team_rush`, and the scorer and the
  harness both call it. The scorer's output is checked byte-identical before
  and after.
- **Live-faithful inputs.** Each season S is predicted from priors built from
  S-1 by `build_priors.py` (the file the scorer would have had), including the
  dispersions and the carry-yard grid. Nothing is fitted on the season under
  test. The old within-season fit (train weeks 5-8) stays available as
  `--dispersion train`, so earlier results reproduce.
- **Seasons:** 2022-2025, walk-forward. 2022-23 are for tuning; 2024-25 are
  the test seasons, and no setting is chosen on them.
- **Weeks 2-18**, reported as two slices: weeks 2-4 (the early-season
  prior-season blend, flagged unvalidated in the model registry) and 5-18.
- **Markets:** receptions, receiving yards, rushing yards (non-QB).
- **Metrics, per market x season x slice:** CRPS of the model against
  baseline A (the player's own recent rates, no shrinkage), bias (actual/model
  mean and PIT mean), and reliability at synthetic lines. The headline is the
  pooled test seasons with a game-block bootstrap interval.
- **Output:** `reports/yardage_harness.md` and `.json`, regenerated by one
  command.

**Pass criteria for a market to be `live`**, all on the test seasons:
1. beats baseline A on each test season, interval excluding zero;
2. no BIASED flag, pooled (PIT mean within 0.03 of 0.5, actual/model mean
   within 5%);
3. **width**: outcomes outside the model's p10-p90 within 0.20 +/- 0.03,
   pooled (each player-week counted once);
4. **calibration**: every 60-90% reliability bucket on weeks 5-18 within 3
   points of its realized rate, both sides.

Criteria 3-4 were added after the first run (user decision, 2026-09-24): all
three markets passed 1-2, yet 23-31% of outcomes fell outside the 10-90 range
and an 85% Over won about 77-80%. Right on average but too narrow overstates
every edge. The harness reports; it promotes nothing by itself.

**Result (after the code review's live-faithfulness fixes):** all three
markets pass 1-2 and fail the width/calibration bar. Receptions 26.1% outside
p10-p90, receiving yards 23.0% (width at the edge, calibration off 4 points),
rushing yards 32.1%; an 85% Over wins 75-81%. The miss is
worst on the low side (players bust more often than the model allows).

## Later steps, each its own PR, each judged by this harness

2. **Width first** (user decision, 2026-09-24) -- DONE, props-v1.20: target-share
   and carry-share variation plus per-game yards per carry; all three markets pass
   the four checks on 2024-25 (reports/width_tuning.md, reports/yardage_harness.md).
   The setting VALUES were chosen on 2022-23 alone; WHICH knobs to try was decided after
   the first harness run, which had shown every season (tune and test) too narrow.
   Candidate causes, each tested
   against the version before it: a player's share of targets or carries is
   fixed within a game (real shares swing week to week -- a
   Dirichlet-multinomial split); yards per carry and per catch have no
   game-level swing; no allowance for leaving a game early. Tuned on 2022-23
   only, judged on 2024-25.
3. Rushing yards: opponent run-defense adjustment, QB rushing (kneel-downs
   count at the book). -- DONE, props-v1.21: the run-defense adjustment already
   existed and measures as helping; QB rushing is simulated (own carry grid,
   kneel-downs by spread, own width settings) and priced, and passes. Running
   backs miss the calibration limit by 0.4 points; the user judged it noise
   (DECISIONS #100). Next fix found on the way: backs run ~4% low because shares
   summing past 1 are scaled down. A rescaling fix was tuned and tested and did
   not beat the live model on 2024-25 (DECISIONS #103); the bias stays below the
   5% limit and the diagnosis is recorded for the next attempt.
4. QB passing yards from the team simulation (the team's receiving yards are
   the QB's passing yards before sacks), plus who is under center. It inherits
   step 2.
5. The Vegas-line environment (`market_fit`) and a market blend layer, tested
   on two seasons instead of one.

Follow-up outside these steps: the slate's must-win pick (`score_week.py`,
`CAL_MARKETS`) prefers receptions and receiving yards as the calibrated
markets. Since step 2 rushing yards passes the same bar; whether it joins
`CAL_MARKETS` is a separate decision about the pick rule.

Each change is tuned on 2022-23 only and reported on 2024-25, against the
version before it, like the TD model.
