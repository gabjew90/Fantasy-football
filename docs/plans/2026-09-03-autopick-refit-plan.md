# Plan: autopick-seat refit of the survival model, done as a measured study (2026-09-03 → 09-04)

## In plain English: what we are building and why it matters

**The problem.** Before every pick the engine asks "if I wait, will this player still be here at my next turn?" and answers with a percentage. That percentage drives the cost-of-waiting number that ranks the picks. In the three mock rooms tonight those percentages were too optimistic: players the engine gave a 30 to 70 percent chance of surviving were almost always gone. The engine was waiting one turn too long on players it could have had.

**The cause.** The engine models each rival seat as a person picking near the player's average draft position. For a seat Yahoo has switched to autopick it assumed an even tighter version of that person. The data says the opposite in a specific way: an autopick seat does not pick near ADP at all; about a third of the time it takes exactly the top player on Yahoo's own default ranking list, which is a different ordering from ADP, and the rest of the time it looks like a person. Our engine never had Yahoo's list, so it could not see this.

**What gets built, in five pieces.**
1. **Eyes on Yahoo's list.** The draft page already holds Yahoo's default rank for every player. The driver will record it, along with how quickly each rival pick arrived after the turn opened (an autopick fires instantly; a person takes seconds). That gives the engine the ranking autopick seats actually follow and a clean way to tell an autopick from a person who happened to be flagged away.
2. **A pick-by-pick dataset.** Every rival pick from every logged room, with who was available at that moment, what that seat still needed, and where the taken player ranked by ADP and by Yahoo's list. This is the raw material; it also produces a one-page report you can read.
3. **A fitted rival model.** Instead of guessing knob values, we fit them to the observed picks: for an autopick seat, how often it walks Yahoo's list versus picks like a person, and how noisy the person-like part is. The fit reports how sure it is of each number, and we compare model shapes on rooms the fit never saw.
4. **A small engine change, off by default.** The simulation gains an "autopick seat walks Yahoo's list with probability p" behaviour and a Yahoo-rank column on the board. Nothing changes until the study says the new numbers are better.
5. **A gate before it ships.** Four checks written down in advance: the fitted model predicts held-out picks better; the survival percentages become honest on rooms it never saw; no regression on the replay of the two archived drafts; and two brand-new mock rooms drafted live with the new numbers must score better than the old numbers would have. Pass all four and the new numbers become the default before Saturday. Fail any and Saturday runs on today's numbers, with the study recorded.

**What it changes for you on draft night.** In a room of nine humans, little: the human model already held up in the 70 to 100 percent range. The change matters the moment any league-mate goes idle and Yahoo flips them to autopick for the rest of the draft, which happened to two thirds of the seats in the bot-heavy mock. From then on the engine would know that seat will grab the next name on Yahoo's list, take the players that seat is about to take one turn earlier, and stop reporting 60 percent odds on players that are really 25 percent. The cost of being wrong today is one player per affected turn taken a pick late, not a bad pick; the fix is worth having, and it is worth having only if the numbers prove it, which is what the gate is for.

**What it costs.** About a day of build and fitting today, two mock rooms tomorrow of roughly 25 minutes each, and nothing on Saturday morning beyond the steps already planned.

## Context

Three instrumented Yahoo mock rooms on 2026-09-02 (rooms 10531886, 10532940, 10534350; reports/mocks/mock24..26_*_scrutiny.md) agree that the engine's survival estimates are overconfident when Yahoo autopick seats are in the room: shown 30-50% observed 0-57%, 50-70% observed 22-32%, 70-90% observed 40-77%, 90-100% observed 81-94%, worst in the room with the most away seats. Two read-only diagnostics tonight locate the cause:

- Rank of the taken player among the players still available, by our board's ADP (Yahoo average-pick): away-at-pick seats n=108, median 9th, top-1 only 5%, below 10th 49%; human seats n=269, median 6th, top-1 13%. The current model assumes the opposite: an autopick seat is *tighter* than a human around ADP (`autopick_sigma_scale 0.5`). Wrong-signed relative to ADP, which is exactly how the simulation ends up sparing the mid-ranked players we target.
- Yahoo's draft-room store exposes per player `o_rank` (Yahoo's default overall rank), `average-pick` (ADP) and `psr_rank`. Measured against `o_rank`, away-at-pick picks are top-1 32% (vs 6% by ADP): about a third are an exact walk of Yahoo's default list (a true autopick), the rest scatter like humans. The `away` flag flickers on and off for some seats (connection status), so it is an impure autopick label; Yahoo's pick records carry no auto flag. Yahoo's autopick fires the instant a turn opens, so a timing label is available to any future room the driver watches.

User decisions (2026-09-03): take effect before Saturday's league draft IF the gates pass; 2 more mock rooms as a true forward test; autopick seats first, human knobs flip only if the human-only view passes on its own. Rules from CLAUDE.md apply: pre-register every threshold in DECISIONS before running, measure before deciding, a failing gate means the default does not move.

The three days: today (09-03) instrument + dataset + fit + pre-registration, 09-04 forward rooms + decision, 09-05 morning draft-day steps only (ADP re-scrape, pre-rank load, board rebuild) — no refitting on draft day.

## What ships (all behind knobs that default to today's behaviour)

### 1. Instrumentation (driver + bridge + board) — `scripts/draft_driver.js`, `scripts/yahoo_bridge.py`, `scripts/export_board_json.py`, `draftkit/market.py`
- `storeState()` (draft_driver.js ~487-523): each drafted entry gains `o_rank`, `avg_pick`, `psr_rank` from `players.byId`; the driver stamps each NEWLY seen pick with `seen_at` (ISO), `clock_left` (`countdown.seconds` at first sight), `poll_gap_ms` (time since the previous poll), and `away_teams_at` (the away set at that instant). Timing label rule, pre-registered: `instant` if `clock_left >= 27` of 30 AND `poll_gap_ms <= 2000`; `human` if `clock_left <= 20`; else `unknown`. (Chrome throttles the hidden tab; a late-seen pick must not read as instant.)
- One full players snapshot per room at injection (`id, name, pos, team, o_rank, average_pick, psr_rank`), POSTed to a new bridge route `/players` (same `_save_named` pattern as `/trail`, `data/logs/mocks/players_<room>.json`). It is also saved as `data/external/yahoo_rank.keefamania.csv` (name, pos, yahoo_rank) by a small script `scripts/yahoo_rank_from_snapshot.py`; `draftkit/market.py` joins it exactly like `yahoo_adp` (league-scoped file, loud warning if unscoped) into a new board column `yahoo_rank`; `export_board_json.py` carries it as `yr`; `yahoo_bridge.load_players` and `engine_parity.load_board` carry it as `yahoo_rank`. Absent file → column null, engine falls back to `adp` (DATA MISSING, said in the bridge banner).
- `trailDump` gains `players_snapshot_ref` and the per-pick fields above; `mock_scrutiny.py` prints the per-pick label and o_rank next to each rival pick in a new "Rival picks" table.
- Tests: fixture `tests/fixtures/yahoo/store-snapshot-expanded.json` (or the existing store fixture) with `o_rank` fields; node tests for the timing label (instant / human / unknown by clock and gap), for the snapshot payload, and for `yahoo_rank` riding through `load_board`/`load_players`.

### 2. Pick-level dataset — new `scripts/pick_dataset.py`
One row per RIVAL pick across every logged room: `room, room_type, pick_no, round, seat, seat_label, player, pos, yahoo_rank, adp, rank_among_available_by_yrank, rank_among_available_by_adp, rank_among_NEED_FITTING_by_yrank, needs_before (that seat's open starter slots), adp_snapshot_date, adp_drift_flag`.
- Sources and labels: Yahoo rooms with a sidecar (3 today + the new ones): `seat_label` from `away_teams × team_id` at the nearest preceding call (never from `away_slots`, whose team→slot map is empty for a seat until it has picked — round-1 autopicks were being labelled human); new rooms additionally carry the timing label (`instant` / `human` / `unknown`). Older Yahoo trails (4) and email rooms (5): `end_away` / `unknown`. Sleeper mocks: `bot`. Omnibeta real draft: `human`. Our own seat: `ours`, excluded from every fit (when WE are flagged away Yahoo walks OUR pre-rank list).
- Available pool at each pick from the room's own pick order (trail); needs from `snake.starter_needs` over that seat's prior picks with the league's slots (`engine_parity.league_shape`); `yahoo_rank` from the room's players snapshot when present, else from `data/external/yahoo_rank.keefamania.csv`, else null.
- ADP drift: for the three existing rooms, mark players whose Yahoo ADP moved > 10 picks between the Aug-31 scrape and the fresh one as `unscoreable` (excluded from the likelihood, counted, never treated as misses); sigma is reported with and without them.
- `--report` writes `reports/rival_picks.md`: per seat label × ranking source (yrank vs adp) the rank-of-taken histogram (1..10, 10+), top-1/top-3 shares, and the same under the starters-first need filter. **Need-rule check before any fit:** if the top-1-by-yrank share of `instant`/away picks does not rise under the starters-first filter, the list being walked is not `o_rank` (personal pre-ranks) and the list component becomes a tight Gaussian in `o_rank` instead of a one-hot.

### 3. Model and fit — new `scripts/rival_fit.py`
- Forms compared per seat class, likelihood = multinomial over the available pool at each pick:
  (i) Gaussian in ADP, sigma(round) as today (`sigma_early → sigma_late` linear);
  (ii) Gaussian in `o_rank`;
  (iii) label-conditional mixture: latent class z ∈ {list, human}; `P(z=list | label) = pi_label` for labels instant / away / human / unknown; list component = one-hot on the lowest-`o_rank` alive player that fits an open starter slot (or the tight-Gaussian variant if the need-rule check says so); human component = Gaussian in ADP with the shared human sigma(round) and need multipliers (`need_damp`, `qb_filled_damp`, `kdef_early_damp`) as in `simulate_survival`. The class is marginalised per pick, so no EM.
- Estimation: direct likelihood on a coarse grid (pi in 0.1 steps; `sigma_early ∈ {4,6,8,10}`, `sigma_late ∈ {15,21,27,35}`; `autopick_sigma_scale ∈ {0.75,1.0,1.5,2.0}`; `need_damp ∈ {.15,.30,.45}`), 1-D profiles with Wilks 90% CIs for every pi and sigma. Report `pi_instant` (expected ≥ 0.8; if not, `o_rank` is not the list), `pi_away` (the deployable value, since the live signal IS the away flag), `pi_human` (expected small, nonzero). Sample-size honesty in the report: SE(pi) ≈ 4 pts at n≈120 exact-hit trials; SE(log sigma) ≈ 8%.
- Selection: held-out leave-one-room-out pick-level log-likelihood per pick (not AIC). Fit rooms: the 3 sidecar rooms (+ any room drafted before the freeze); unknown-label rooms contribute to sigma only.
- Output `reports/rival_fit.md` + `.json`: the chosen form, the point on the grid, profiles, LORO table per seat class.

### 4. Engine change — `draftkit/urgency.py`, `draftkit/tracker.py`, `config.yaml`
- New knob `autopick_list_prob` (default 0.0 = today, byte-identical) and pool key `yrank` (tracker fills it from the board's `yahoo_rank`, falling back to `adp`). Inside the sim loop the existing reach uniform is reused: `u = rng.random(); reaching = u < reach_prob; walking = autopick[i] and u < autopick_list_prob`; when walking, `like` becomes a one-hot on the alive, need-eligible player with the lowest `yrank` (fallback: lowest yrank over alive; then adp) and `rng.choice` still runs against it — the draw count per rival per sim is unchanged, so every paired-seed test keeps passing.
- `autopick_sigma_scale` becomes whatever the fit says for the non-walking component (evidence points ≥ 1.0, i.e. human-like or wider); `autopick_need_damp` fitted on the same grid.
- Knobs in `Tracker.ENGINE_KNOBS` / class defaults / `config.yaml` engine block; `engine_parity.make_tracker` accepts `away_slots` via overrides (already possible) and `survival_refit` passes each Yahoo room's per-state away set from the sidecar so the autopick branch is actually exercised in replays (today it never is).
- Tests: `autopick_list_prob=0` equals the no-arg call bit for bit; `=1` with one autopick rival takes the top-yrank need-fitting player (its survival 0, everyone else 1); human seats identical at 0 and 1 under one seed; yrank fallback to adp; reach uniform still consumed once per rival.

### 5. Validation harness — `scripts/survival_refit.py`, new `scripts/yahoo_trail_replay.py`
- `survival_refit`: reads the sidecar's `away_teams`-derived per-pick away set for Yahoo rooms; the autopick STAGE is uncommented and extended (`autopick_list_prob`, `autopick_sigma_scale`, `autopick_need_damp`); LORO by room; per-room Yahoo ADP snapshot when a room has one (new rooms), else the board's; calibration buckets get **cluster-bootstrap 90% CIs** over (room, window) and an effective-n; the bar is restated (see gates). Tests for `state_rows`, `logloss`, `evaluate`, the bootstrap (none exist today).
- `yahoo_trail_replay.py`: adapts a trail (`data/logs/mocks/mock_<room>.json`) into `slot_replay.replay`'s log format (`pick_no, slot, player, round` via `snake.pick_to_round_slot`, names matched by the shared key), passes `away_slots` per state, re-drafts OUR seat with the engine at each of the 10 slots at a knob point, grades `lineup_points` on projected points. `--set knob=value` A/B like `slot_replay`.

### 6. Pre-registration — DECISIONS.md #35, written BEFORE any fit runs
Forms, labels, grid, and these gates:
- G1 (primary, autopick): LORO pick-level log-likelihood per pick for the `away`/`instant` class improves over form (i) at today's knobs; the 90% CI of `pi_away` excludes 0.
- G2 (deployment): survival Bernoulli log-loss at the fitted point ≤ current, LORO, in the pooled view AND the autopick view; calibration: in no bucket with effective n ≥ 30 may the cluster-bootstrap 90% CI of (observed − predicted) exclude 0 in the pooled or autopick view. Human view: reported; the human knobs flip only if the human view's log-loss also improves and its buckets pass; otherwise human knobs stay (per the user's scope decision).
- G3 (outcome, no-regression): `slot_replay` on both Sleeper leagues, fitted vs current, mean lineup points not worse (autopick knobs are inert there; this guards the human side). `yahoo_trail_replay` on the Yahoo rooms with sidecars, all 10 slots, fitted vs current: mean not worse.
- G4 (forward test): after the freeze, TWO new mock rooms drafted live with the fitted knobs (instrumented; fresh ADP and `o_rank` captured that day). Offline, both knob sets are scored on those rooms' realised states: fitted pooled survival log-loss lower than current, AND the 30-70% bucket miss smaller pooled, AND no single room worse by more than 0.01 log-loss. These two rooms also count toward the clean-room rule.
- Honesty clauses: the three-room numbers establish the SIGN of the miss, not its size; bucket CIs at n≈15 cannot fail an 8-point bar (binomial SE 7.7 at p=0.9), hence the CI-based bar; `autopick_list_prob` and `pi_away` are reported at grid precision; no parameter is claimed identified beyond its profile CI; fit and forward rooms share the board but not the ADP date (drift stated per room).
- Decision rule: G1 ∧ G2 ∧ G3 ∧ G4 → `autopick_list_prob`, `autopick_sigma_scale`, `autopick_need_damp` flip in `config.yaml` + Tracker defaults, `yahoo_rank` column ships on the board. Any fail → knobs stay, the study is recorded, `yahoo_rank` still ships (data, not a decision). Human knobs: only on the human sub-gate.
